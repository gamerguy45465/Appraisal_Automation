## Caption

Buying in Reno or Las Vegas? Keep an eye on bonds—and your budget.

Higher bond yields can keep mortgage rates elevated. The 10-year Treasury yield reached 4.83 percent on September 9. Freddie Mac’s latest national 30-year fixed mortgage average was 6.71 percent on September 3, up from 6.66 percent the week before.

Local markets tell different stories. August’s single-family median sale price was $620,000 in the Reno-area Washoe report, up 5.1 percent from a year earlier. In Southern Nevada, it was $475,000, down 1.0 percent.

Your next step: Shop the home price and the monthly payment together. Talk with Guild Mortgage about a budget that fits your goals.

Market update as of September 9, 2026. National mortgage survey average, not a loan offer. Your rate may differ. Reno-area figures reflect SNR’s Washoe County reporting area, which excludes Incline Village. Las Vegas-area figures cover Southern Nevada. Sources: U.S. Treasury, Freddie Mac, Sierra Nevada REALTORS® and Las Vegas REALTORS®.
