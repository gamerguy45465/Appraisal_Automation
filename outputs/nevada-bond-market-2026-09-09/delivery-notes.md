# Nevada market social graphics

- Portrait PNG: **1080 × 1350**, suitable for a portrait feed post.
- Square PNG: **1080 × 1080**, suitable for a square feed post.
- Matching SVG files preserve text and layout as editable vector sources.
- The caption, alt text and cited research brief accompany the graphics.

## Adobe connector limitation

Adobe initialization failed twice with MCP internal error `-32603`. Its template-search tool returned the same error. No Adobe Express document or Adobe download link was created. The delivered graphics were instead composed and rendered locally. The source files can be retained for later design work; import behavior in Adobe has not been tested. Nothing was posted to social media.

## Brand and verification notes

The Guild Mortgage marketing skill supplied the copy, color and logo-placement guidance. Layout colors use the supplied digital values: blue #262A82, yellow #F9C606, white #FFFFFF, black #191A1D and neutral #F6EDE4. Arial is a practical typeface choice, not a claim about an official Guild font. Thin rules use the documented gray tint #E8EBEB.

The official logotype PNG was downloaded unchanged from [Guild’s branch-site asset](https://branches.guildmortgage.com/logo.png), observed on [an official Guild Las Vegas page](https://branches.guildmortgage.com/nv/lasvegas/jacob-orth-685-lvjo.html). Its original color values and proportions are preserved. It is placed at 220 pixels wide in the portrait and 190 pixels in the square, above the 55-pixel minimum, with clear space exceeding twice the triangle height. Logo artwork is not reconstructed from the brand-guide PDF.

The company identifiers and Equal Housing Opportunity wording were checked against [Guild’s official homepage](https://www.guildmortgage.com/). No individual loan officer identity, license, contact details or loan terms were invented. The files have not received a Guild legal approval.

Both exported PNGs were visually inspected for layout, text clipping and legibility. Dimensions were read from their actual image metadata. Calculated text contrast ratios: blue on white 12.10:1, black on white 17.40:1, blue on neutral 10.46:1 and black on neutral 15.04:1. These calculations verify those color pairs, not overall accessibility certification.

The August SNR dashboard was visually verified against its original PDF. Regional labels and the Incline Village coverage note are retained to avoid representing the figures as city-only or complete-county statistics. Data dates differ by release and are shown on the graphics. Update time-sensitive values before reusing this post on a later date.
